# MakeaMove — Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
Singles (Nigerian/African diaspora focus, 20s–30s) joining a game-show-style
dating app. They arrive curious and a little nervous; the product's job is to
turn the anxiety of dating into the fun of being a contestant. Used on phones,
often at night; the prototype also renders as a framed phone showcase on
desktop.

### Brand Personality
Primetime, warm, suspenseful. "Millionaire's stagecraft, Family Feud's heart."
The interface is a lit TV stage: drama in service of warmth, never at the
user's expense.

### Aesthetic Direction
Governed by **docs/design-language.md ("PRIMETIME")** — derived from
playthroughs of Arkadium's Family Feud and Who Wants to Be a Millionaire.
Key commitments:
- Dark stage screens are *lit* (pink neon structure, amber bulb runs, LED
  dot-matrix walls, spotlight washes); light `paper` screens stay calm utility.
- Signature shape: the chamfered **Ticket** lozenge for questions, options,
  and stakes.
- Pink (`--move`) = neon/romance; amber (`--bulb`) = stakes, scores, locked
  choices; green (`--pass`) = reveals. A player's own choice is never red.
- Commitment choreography: armed → locked (amber) → beat (600ms) → reveal.
- Outcomes get ceremony screens (dimmed stage, gold verdict, one CTA), not
  toasts.
- Type: Big Shoulders Display = announcer (caps, tracked); Hanken Grotesk =
  host (sentence case). Scores are big ticking gold numerals.

### Design Principles
1. Light is the brand material — an unlit dark screen is unfinished.
2. One signature shape (the Ticket), repeated at every scale.
3. Gold means stakes; pink means romance; never swap them.
4. Choreograph commitment; suspense before reveal.
5. Never shame the player — reveal truth, keep dignity.

### Constraints
Single self-contained HTML files (inline CSS + vanilla JS, Google Fonts only).
Reuse `:root` tokens in index.html; extend them per design-language.md §3.
