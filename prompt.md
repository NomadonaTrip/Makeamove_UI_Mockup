Additional questions should be introduced during sync game and not before. This is because users may not be sufficiently stimulated to ask the right questions until they're in the game.
When the user asks an additional question, those questions are tagged important by default. The user is able to lower the importance value of each question manually.
Scheduling should work thus - each user suggests 7 dates within a 2 week period. The closest mutually selected date is automatically selected by the system.
When either party makes their date suggestions, the other party is notified that suggestions have been made.
When the date is confirmed, the process is repeated for the time - each party makes suggestions of 6 time blocks within a 12 hour period. The earliest mutual time suggested is selected by the system.
In the profile page, the candidates should be presented one by one. A maximum of 3 matched candidates are presented daily. However, if the customer doesn't have any matches, we give them the closest candidates to their preferences.
Instead of percentages, we could use range of fits e.g. very close fit, close fit, neutral fit, not so great fit, distant fit. THe model should come up with a more appropriate naming for the scale.
