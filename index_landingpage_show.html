House rules

The house lights are up. This is how we keep the show safe, and fair to everyone on it.

  • ID checked

    Every contestant verifies a government ID with a live selfie before they see a single match.

  • Single, and over 18

    Under-18s can't sign up. Married people can't join, and anyone can report someone who slips through.

  • Rooted questions, asked up front

    Built for Nigerians and Africans at home and abroad. Genotype, state of origin and faith come up before the first date, not during it.

  • Manners count

    Answering on time and turning up raise your standing. Ghosting lowers it. Only you ever see your own penalties.

  • An alarm on every date

    One button shares your exact location if a meeting ever feels wrong.

  • Nobody is shamed

    A Stay is information, not a verdict. There are no red crosses on this stage.
