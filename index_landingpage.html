How the show runs

From your first question to your first date, in four steps.

  1. 1

    Get in

    Answer ten questions about who you are and where you're rooted, then verify your ID with a quick selfie. Single and 18 or over only.

  2. 2

    Meet your matches

    Up to three a day, shown one at a time. They're ordered by how well you fit, and by how well each person treats others.

  3. 3

    Play the Show

    Book a live show with a match. You take turns: one answers, the other reacts Move or Stay. You both need 70% to pass. Can't find a time? Play the board over 72 hours instead.

  4. 4

    Call, then date

    Pass together and you set up a video call. If it goes well, we'll help you both pick a day, a time, and a venue that's fair to both of you.

Everyone on stage is who they say they are

A show only works if the contestants are real. So we check, before anyone meets you.

A Stay is not a rejection

It means you want different things, and it's better to find out now. Nobody is told off, and nobody sees a red cross next to their name.

  • ID checked with a live selfie

    Every member verifies a government ID before they see a single match.

  • Single and 18 or over, checked at the door

    Under-18s can't sign up, married people can't join, and members can report anyone who slips through.

  • How you treat people counts

    Answering on time and turning up raises your standing. Ghosting lowers it and moves you down the list. Only you ever see your own penalties.

  • An alarm on every date

    One button shares your exact location if a meeting ever feels wrong.
